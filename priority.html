How the ranking works

Score = 50% how many questions the topic had in total + 30% how many in the last 5 papers (the exam drifts, so recent counts more) + 20% how many of the papers it appeared in at all (consistency). Tiers go down the ranked list by cumulative share of questions: Tier 1 until 50% of all classified questions are covered, Tier 2 until 80%, Tier 3 the rest. ≈ marks/paper is the average questions per paper × 2 marks. Exact repeats of the same question are rare (about 0.5% in Paper 2), so this ranks concepts, not questions to memorise.
