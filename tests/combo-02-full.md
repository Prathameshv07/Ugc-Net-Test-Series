## Q33
<div class="stem"><p>Which of the following contains a formal fallacy?</p></div>

## Q33 - Options
(A) All A are B. Some B are C. Therefore, all A are C.
(B) All A are B. All B are C. Therefore, all A are C.
(C) Some A are B. Some B are C. Therefore, some A may be C.
(D) All A are B. No B are C. Therefore, no A are C.

## Q33 - Hint
**Answer:** A  
**Topic:** Logical Reasoning – Syllogism (Fallacies)

"All A are B; some B are C ⇒ all A are C" – the middle term **B** is not distributed in either premise, so A and C need not overlap. This is the **fallacy of the undistributed middle**, a formal fallacy.

(B) and (D) are valid syllogisms; (C) only claims "may be", which makes no invalid claim.
