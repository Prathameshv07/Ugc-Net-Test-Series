## Q11
<div class="stem"><p>In school, 450 students appeared in an exam for Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. The following data is known: 167 passed in all three subjects.60 failed in all three subjects.175 failed in Physics.199 failed in Mathematics. 191 failed in Chemistry.62 passed in Physics only.48 passed in Mathematics only.52 passed in Chemistry only. How many students failed in exactly one subject?</p></div>

## Q11 - Options
(A) 45
(B) 61
(C) 75
(D) 88

## Q11 - Hint
**Answer:** B  
**Topic:** Mathematical Reasoning – Sets

**Step 1:** Passed at least one subject = 450 − 60 (failed all) = **390**.  
**Step 2:** Passed exactly one = 62 + 48 + 52 = **162**. Passed all three = **167**.  
**Step 3:** Passed exactly two = 390 − 162 − 167 = **61**.

Passing exactly two subjects = failing exactly one subject → **61**.
