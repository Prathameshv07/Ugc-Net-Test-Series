## Q1 - Options
(A) 80%
(B) 120%
(C) 100%
(D) 90%

## Q1 - Hint
**Answer:** C  
**Topic:** Data Interpretation

Total = 600. Work out each class first:

| Class | Students | Boys | Girls |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (20%, 3:2) | 120 | 72 | 48 |
| B (12%, 3:1) | 72 | 54 | 18 |
| C (16%, 5:3) | 96 | 60 | 36 |
| D (15%, 8:7) | 90 | 48 | 42 |
| E (21%, 4:3) | 126 | 72 | 54 |
| F (16%, 1:1) | 96 | 48 | 48 |

P = 72 − 48 = 24; Q = 60 − 36 = 24 → P is **100%** of Q.

<div class="stem"><p>Consider a binary tree whose preorder traversal is KLNMPRQST and inorder traversal is NLKPRMSQT. What will be the postorder traversal ?</p></div>
