**Answer:** B  
**Topic:** Data Interpretation

## Q11
<div class="stem"><p>The proposition "No birds are mammals" is equivalent to which of the following propositions?</p> <p>(a) All mammals are non-birds.</p> <p>(b) Some non-mammals are birds.</p> <p>(c) No non-birds are mammals.</p> <p>(d) Some birds are non-mammals.</p> <p>Select the correct answer from the options given below:</p></div>

## Q11 - Options
(A) (a), (b), (c) and (d)
(B) (a) and (c) only
(C) (b) and (d) only
(D) (a) only

## Q11 - Hint
**Answer:** B  
**Topic:** Logical Reasoning – Immediate Inference

"No birds are mammals" (E):

- **Converse:** No mammals are birds ✔
- **Obverse of that:** All mammals are non-birds → **(a)** ✔
- (b), (d) – "some …" forms follow by limitation, so they are not *equivalent*.
- **(c)** "No non-birds are mammals" – this would mean every mammal is a bird. That is **not** equivalent (dogs are non-birds and mammals).

> **⚠ Note:** Only **(a)** is strictly equivalent, which gives option **(D)**. The key's (B) wrongly includes (c).
